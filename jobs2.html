My Profile

I started coding in school, where I built a simple HTML-based adventure game and a Java-MySQL banking app. That curiosity grew into a career spanning ERP system, VR development, and data analytics.
I’ve worked with startups, research labs, and enterprises — always solving real problems. At Softech ERP Solutions, I built an ERP system from scratch for Intime Ltd, streamlining workflows for 500+ employees. At Dublin City University, I collaborated with Dr. Enda Donlon and Dr. Peter Tiernan to design a VR classroom simulation that captured and analyzed student interactions in real time. With Fineax LLC, I automated DICOM data anonymization pipelines for medical data using Python, PyDicom, and AWS.
My journey has been entirely self-taught through hands-on projects, YouTube tutorials, and open- source contributions. I’ve built 20+ GitHub repositories, ranging from VR engines and automation tools to data analytics workflows and multiplayer game prototypes. I thrive on challenges that combine creativity and problem-solving — whether it’s optimizing databases, designing immersive learning environments, or exploring game mechanics. Currently, I’m excited to keep building in the areas of software development, VR/game design, and data-driven applications, while collaborating with teams that value innovation, curiosity, and impact
