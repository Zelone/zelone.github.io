About me

I'm Jitesh Jhawar, also Zelone on GitHub. I'm a self-taught developer who likes taking a product from a rough requirement all the way to something customers use every day.

How I got here

I started building software during my B.Tech in Computer Science at SRM in Chennai: a hospital management system at a hackathon, PL/SQL migrations as a database intern, and full-stack work at an early-stage startup. After graduating I was the sole developer of an ERP serving a nationwide company's 500+ employees.

I moved to Ireland for an M.Sc. in Data Analytics at Dublin City University, where my thesis was on summarising financial narratives. I stayed on as a Research Assistant, building a VR classroom simulation and its data backend for education researchers, while freelancing on medical-imaging pipelines for Fineax.

Today I'm back in Kolkata as Lead Developer at Trafasa, managing the development team behind Trafasa.ai and Proquro.ai. Most of my time goes into AI-first workflows: agents that answer supplier questions and triage disputes, running on-prem on open models.

How I work

A lot of my work is the unglamorous part that makes software dependable: queues and retries for large batch jobs, checksum validation on data pipelines, and role-based access control.

Outside work I tinker with game engines, Unity, and automation scripts. Some of that is on my projects page.
